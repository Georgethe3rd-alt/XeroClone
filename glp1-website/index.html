The Science

What Are GLP-1 Medications?

GLP-1 receptor agonists are breakthrough medications that mimic a natural hormone your body produces to regulate blood sugar and appetite.

🧬

Reduces Hunger

GLP-1 signals your brain that you're full, naturally reducing appetite and calorie intake without willpower battles.

🩺

Controls Blood Sugar

Stimulates insulin release and slows digestion to keep blood glucose stable, ideal for type 2 diabetes management.

❤️

Protects the Heart

Clinical trials show GLP-1 medications significantly reduce risk of major cardiovascular events in at-risk patients.

⚡

Boosts Metabolism

Slows gastric emptying so you feel fuller longer, naturally reducing total caloric intake throughout the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

GLP-1 medications are generally prescribed for adults with a BMI of 27+ with at least one weight-related condition (like diabetes, high blood pressure, or high cholesterol), or a BMI of 30+ regardless of other conditions. A licensed provider will review your health history to confirm eligibility.

Most patients begin noticing reduced appetite within the first 1–2 weeks. Meaningful weight loss (5–10 lbs) typically occurs within the first month. Maximum results are usually seen at 6–12 months of consistent treatment, with patients losing an average of 15–22% of body weight.

The most common side effects are nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and constipation — especially when starting or increasing your dose. These typically subside within a few weeks. Our providers use a gradual dose titration schedule to minimize discomfort. Serious side effects are rare but are monitored throughout treatment.

Coverage varies widely by insurance plan and indication. Many plans cover GLP-1s for type 2 diabetes (like Ozempic), while weight loss coverage is more limited. Our care team will help you navigate insurance options, prior authorization, and manufacturer savings programs to minimize your out-of-pocket cost.

Yes. GLP-1 medications can be stopped at any time. However, stopping medication often leads to weight regain as appetite returns. Many patients transition to a maintenance dose for long-term weight management. Your provider will work with you on a sustainable plan tailored to your goals.

GLP-1 medications come in a prefilled auto-injector pen. You inject under the skin of your abdomen, thigh, or upper arm once weekly (or daily for liraglutide). The needle is very thin — most patients describe it as a brief pinch. We provide detailed injection instructions and our team is available to walk you through your first dose.
